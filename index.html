Who We Are

Building the
Next Generation
of Footballers

TFP Academy was born from a simple belief — every child deserves the chance to play football, regardless of where they grow up. We bring professional-standard coaching directly into local communities across the city.

Our certified coaches focus on technical skill, physical fitness, tactical understanding, and above all, a deep love for the beautiful game. Whether your child is picking up a football for the first time or looking to take their game to the next level, TFP Academy has a pathway for them.
