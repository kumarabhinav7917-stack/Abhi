About Us

TFP Academy is passionate about making football accessible to every child, regardless of background. We believe the beautiful game has the power to build character, discipline, teamwork, and a love for sport. From grassroots coaching to competitive tournaments, we are on a mission to bring the game to every neighbourhood.

What We Offer

⚽

Football Coaching

Professional coaching sessions tailored for all age groups and skill levels. Our certified coaches focus on technique, fitness, and game intelligence.

🏆

Organising Tournaments

We organise competitive and friendly tournaments that give players real match experience, build team spirit, and bring communities together through football.

🎓

After School Coaching Partnerships

We partner with schools to deliver structured after-school football programs, helping students stay active, build confidence, and develop a lifelong love of the sport.
